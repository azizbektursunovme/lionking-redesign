# Sahifalar xaritasi va bloklar

```
/                    Bosh sahifa
├── /services/       Xizmatlar (#last-mile, #long-haul, #dispatch, #freight-management)
├── /fleet/          Park
├── /careers/        Ishga kirish (#positions, #apply)
├── /about/          Kompaniya haqida
├── /contact/        Narx so'rash formasi + kontakt (#contact)
├── /privacy-policy/ Privacy Policy
├── /terms-of-service/ Terms of Service
├── /credits/        Vaqtinchalik suratlar mualliflari
├── /styleguide/     Dizayn tizimi (ichki, indekslanmaydi)
└── 404              Brendli
```

Yuqori qator (≥1024px): bosh ofis, email, "Dispatch 24/7" (USDOT/MC kelsa shu yerda).
Header: logotip, Services, Fleet, Careers, About, Contact, telefon, "Get a quote", kun/tun tugmasi. Tepada shaffof, scroll'da oq (tun rejimida qora).
Telefonda: logotip + kun/tun tugmasi + "Menu"; menyu ichida 5 band, "Get a quote", "Drive with us", "Call".
Footer: logotip, Services (4 havola), Company, Contact, legal havolalar.

## `/`
1. Hero: "Reliable freight. Nationwide delivery.", tavsif, "Get a quote", "Drive with us".
2. Qisqa forma paneli → `/contact/` (ma'lumot to'ldirilgan holda).
3. Raqamlar: 2015, 48 shtat, 1,000+ yetkazish, 24/7.
4. 4 xizmat kartasi, har birida o'z "Learn more" havolasi.
5. Coverage (qora band): mintaqalar, AQSh xaritasi, Columbus, OH belgisi.
6. "Why shippers choose Lion King" (USDOT/MC kelsa shu yerda).
7. CTA: haydovchilar (surat) va "Ready to ship?" (oltin).

## `/services/`
Sarlavha → 4 xizmat bloki (surat + ro'yxat + "Request this service") → 5 qadamli jarayon → sohalar.

## `/fleet/`
Sarlavha → raqamlar (10, 100% GPS, 2022+) → 10 × Freightliner sleeper jihozlari va surat → texnologiya → CTA.

## `/careers/`
Sarlavha → raqamlar → 6 imtiyoz → 3 lavozim ("Apply as …" formada lavozimni tanlaydi) → talablar → ariza formasi + recruiting paneli.

## `/about/`
Sarlavha → raqamlar → missiya, iqtibos, vizyon → 4 qadriyat → CTA.

## `/contact/`
Sarlavha → 2 qadamli forma (yo'nalish, sana, og'irlik, yuk turi, yuk, jihoz; aloqa; ixtiyoriy SMS) → dispatch paneli (telefon, email, manzil, ish vaqti).
